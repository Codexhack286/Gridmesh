CHEATSHEET: HOW TO ANSWER TOUGH QUESTIONS FROM JUDGES
================================================================================

Q1: "How do normal users upload their data every 15 minutes?"
A1: "They don't! Microgrids require 100% autonomous data acquisition. Each prosumer 
     has an IoT Edge Gateway connected to their smart meter via DLMS/COSEM and to 
     their inverter via Modbus RTU. The gateway automatically streams encrypted 
     telemetry via MQTT every 15 minutes. Prosumers only configure their preferences 
     once on a mobile app."

Q2: "Why isn't this a website where people click Buy and Sell?"
A2: "Because in smart grids conforming to IEEE 2030.7 DER standards, dispatch happens 
     every 15 minutes (96 intervals a day). Expecting humans to log in 96 times a day 
     to trade fractions of a kilowatt-hour is physically impossible. Autonomous 
     software agents execute trades on the prosumer's behalf."

Q3: "How do you avoid lookahead data leakage in your ML model?"
A3: "We use strict temporal out-of-sample validation: Days 1 through 23 for training, 
     and Days 24 through 30 for chronological testing. We do not shuffle time-series 
     data. Furthermore, cyclical trigonometric features (sin/cos of the 96 daily ticks) 
     allow the tree ensemble to model diurnal solar transitions without midnight 
     discontinuities."

Q4: "What happens when a node tries to manipulate prices or self-trade?"
A4: "Our Regulation Agent audits every proposed trade before clearing against 5 statutory 
     rules (R-01 price collar, R-02 volume cap, R-03 self-trade wash prevention, 
     R-04 collusion floor, R-05 DT limit). Violations are flagged, logged, and auto-voided 
     before reaching the blockchain ledger."
